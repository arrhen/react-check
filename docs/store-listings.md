## Description
React Check is a free, open-source second pair of eyes for food labels.

Add each person you cook for, with their allergies and intolerances and how serious each one is. Photograph an ingredients list, and React Check reads it on your device and flags anything that matches, such as "CRITICAL - SEVERE ALLERGEN FOR MIA".

- Looks for names and relatives (casein, whey and lactose for milk; every tree nut by name)
- Spots "may contain" and facility warnings and keeps them separate from ingredients
- Handles allergies and intolerances, including dairy and lactose
- Reads English labels and has a first draft for Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese and Japanese labels
- Says "couldn't read this reliably" when a photo isn't clear, instead of guessing
- Private: no account, no tracking, no network requests of its own. Photos are read on your device and the people you add stay on it.

React Check is an aid, not a guarantee. It can misread a label or miss an ingredient, and it can't see cross-contact that isn't printed on the pack. It is not medical advice. Always read the label yourself.
